Backend Developer

We are looking for a skilled Backend Developer to join our growing engineering team.
You will be responsible for designing, building, and maintaining scalable APIs and
services that power our core product.

Requirements:
- Minimum 3 years of professional experience in backend development
- Strong proficiency in Python, with hands-on experience in Django or Flask
- Solid understanding of REST APIs and microservices architecture
- Experience with relational databases such as PostgreSQL or MySQL
- Familiarity with containerization tools like Docker and orchestration with Kubernetes
- Experience deploying and managing services on AWS
- Working knowledge of Git and CI/CD pipelines
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field

Nice to have:
- Experience with MongoDB or other NoSQL databases
- Exposure to Redis for caching
- Familiarity with Linux server environments

Responsibilities:
- Design and implement RESTful APIs used by millions of requests daily
- Collaborate with frontend and product teams to ship new features
- Optimize database queries and improve system performance
- Participate in code reviews and contribute to engineering best practices
- Troubleshoot and resolve production issues in a timely manner
